Useful information about the COCA crypto card

COCA Card in Ukraine: which USD and EUR accounts are available?

COCA has a dollar account funded with USDT or USDC and a euro account funded with EURC. This provides a choice of currency for different expenses. Deposit networks depend on the token; the deposit list does not mean the same routes are available for withdrawals.

What cashback does COCA Card offer, and when can I claim it?

In the recorded terms, COCA Starter accrues 1% cashback with a monthly claim cap of $15. Standard offers 3% with a $25 claim cap and requires staking 300 COCA. After a 14-day wait, rewards must be claimed manually, from $1 and at most once per 24 hours. Other bonuses, including subscription rebates, may use the same allowance.

Withdrawing USDC and EURC from COCA: which network and fee apply?

In the scenario I checked, withdrawals use USDC or EURC on Base. In my completed USDC test, the external wallet received exactly the internally credited amount without an additional USDC deduction. This is not a promise of free withdrawals for any token on any network.

COCA balance yield: does 5% APY apply to the entire balance?

No. My app snapshot shows 5% APY and claims from $1, but the documentation has conflicting wording about the eligible balance. Applying the rate to all $1,000 in the scoring model is marked as an assumption. It is a model, rather than measured annual earnings.
