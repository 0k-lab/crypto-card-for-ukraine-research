Useful information about the Keyone crypto card

KeyOne Card: what are the USDT and USDC funding fees?

Under the recorded Free terms, crypto funding costs 0.8% plus a network component. In my full test, 10 USDT resulted in 9.910683 USDC returned; this is the nominal difference for the entire route, rather than an isolated top-up fee. I account for external sender gas separately.

Is KeyOne Premium worthwhile, and does it offer purchase cashback?

I found no numerical KeyOne cashback program in the terms I read. Premium costs €149 per year and reduces the crypto funding fee from 0.8% to 0.33%, plus network costs. I assess its benefit through savings on deposits, rather than purchase rewards. Whether Premium pays for itself depends on the volume of deposits.

Funding KeyOne through SEPA and ACH: what should I check?

KeyOne’s public materials describe SEPA and ACH routes, so funding is not limited to crypto. I have not tested these routes in this research. I therefore make no finding about a specific route’s availability for my account, actual processing time or full funding cost.

What is the fee for withdrawing USDC from KeyOne on Base?

My completed test delivered 9.910683 USDC to the external wallet on Base, matching the internal credit and withdrawal debit exactly. There was no additional USDC deduction at that step. This does not remove the funding cost or establish a free tariff for every future transfer.
