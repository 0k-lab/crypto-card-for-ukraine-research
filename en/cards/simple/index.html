New wallet and Gasless

09.11.2026

Simple documents a required, gradual migration to Dynamic MPC; the cut-off date has not been announced. The card, IBAN, balances and history carry over. The TON deposit address changes after migration. A recovery code is needed if access is lost; the code alone does not grant access without login and verification.

Gasless applies to sending from the self-custody wallet. On the listed routes, network fees are paid in the token sent; there is also paid activation once per network. Both amounts are shown before confirmation. Card top-ups and swaps have separate fees.

Useful information about the Simple crypto card

How do I fund Simple Card with crypto and withdraw the remaining balance?

The route I checked supports direct card funding, so a separate internal transfer before paying is unnecessary. Returning funds followed a different route: card → embedded wallet → external address. I distinguish the two processes so that withdrawal steps are not added to an ordinary purchase.

Does Simple Card offer cashback if Auto Earning is available?

I have not established a permanent cashback program with defined rates and caps in the terms I read. I therefore add no Simple cashback to the purchase-cost calculation. Individual promotions and Auto Earning have different terms and do not replace regular purchase cashback.

What are Simple Gasless fees for USDT and USDC transfers?

No. On supported routes in the new wallet, the network fee can be paid in a stablecoin instead of the native token. Each network requires a separate paid activation. I keep the older Base USDC test without deductions as historical evidence; I have not measured the new wallet’s current full fee.

Simple Wallet: does the TON address change, and how can I recover access?

The Dynamic MPC migration documentation requires keeping the recovery code: recovery after losing access is impossible without it. The TON address for new deposits also changes. These are the documented migration terms; my completed old-wallet test does not verify the new process.
