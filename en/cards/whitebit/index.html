Useful information about the WhiteBIT USD crypto card

WhiteBIT Nóva USD: what are the card payment fees?

The documents checked on November 9, 2026 state 1.5% + $0.20 per transaction, with a possible additional 1.5% conversion charge. The fixed $0.20 particularly affects small purchases. A dollar account alone therefore does not mean low-cost payments.

What is WhiteBIT Nóva cashback: categories, 10% and the $25 cap?

No. In my account snapshot, higher rates depend on selected categories and the merchant’s MCC, with a shared monthly cap of $25. Categories must be selected before purchase. Rewards can be in BTC or WBT; their nominal valuation is not dollars already received.

How do I fund WhiteBIT USD Card from the main exchange balance?

My checked scenario uses the exchange’s shared balance: after funding the main account, a separate manual card transfer was unnecessary. I find that convenient, but the card remains linked to the exchange account. I account for payment fees and withdrawal rules separately.

What did a WhiteBIT Nóva USD payment in UAH cost in my test?

The controlled 158 UAH purchase on October 9, 2026 debited 3.82 USDT for a $3.57 card amount. This is already the measured total debit. The current tariff’s effective date is unknown, so adding its fees again could double-count costs; a current rank requires a new comparable test.
