Useful information about the Trustee Plus crypto card

Trustee Plus: what is my review of the card and its drawbacks?

In my test, Trustee Plus gave me the worst experience among these cards: a separate wallet-to-card transfer, minimum amounts, extra fees and waiting. I found the process slow and insufficiently clear. After this test, I cannot recommend it for everyday payments.

How do I fund the Trustee Plus card with USDT or USDC?

In my completed test, I first funded the card separately from the internal wallet. The card top-up minimum was 10 plus a fee. Public documentation also describes Auto Top-Up, but I have not tested that automatic route and keep it separate from my completed test.

What are the withdrawal fee and minimum in Trustee Plus?

On the routes I checked, the external transfer minimum was 10, with a fee on top; the lowest displayed fee was 0.50. A balance equal to the minimum could therefore be insufficient. I also swapped USDC to USDT and added funds to prepare the withdrawal; completed receipt in the external wallet remains unconfirmed.

Does Trustee Plus offer cashback, and what do Trustee Points provide?

In my records, Trustee Points are internal points for a seasonal prize competition. I have not established a fixed payout in cash or stablecoins. I therefore do not deduct them from purchase costs in the monetary comparison.
