Useful information about the Solid Core crypto card

Is Solid Card suitable for everyday payments in Ukraine?

Solid Card is my current choice for regular use in Ukraine: it has USD and EUR, cashback, and several funding and withdrawal routes. In October 2026, I made 91 merchant payments totalling $1,403.09. This reflects a month of my use, rather than just one controlled payment.

Solid Core cashback: how much did I receive in a month of use?

Yes, my October cashback came to $30. Solid does not include cashback in the statement, so I show this amount separately from purchase debits. The $30 does not establish a guaranteed rate for the next month: the result depends on eligible payments and the Rewards terms.

Can I pay for ChatGPT with crypto through Solid Card?

Yes, the historical tests include a $100 USD ChatGPT payment with $3 displayed cashback under Standard 3%. I have not separately reconciled cashback payout for this particular payment or its full crypto debit. During earlier checks, approving an online purchase required opening Approvals in the card settings, which I find an extra step.

What are the fees for withdrawing USDT and USDC from Solid?

My completed USDT withdrawal test on Fuse had no additional token deduction. For USDC on Fuse, the app also showed no minimum or fee. Other networks showed variable Stargate Bridge and LayerZero charges, so I do not apply the Fuse conditions to every route.
