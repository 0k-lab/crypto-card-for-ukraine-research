First 30 days from issuing the first EUR card; pre-existing EUR cards start from the July 24, 2026 program launch. USD cards start from activation. Higher spending does not restart the period.

At $1 000 in eligible purchases: up to $10 at 1% or $20 at 2%. The full rate applies to purchases up to €100; larger purchases are capped at €1 / €2 cashback each. Exact monthly cashback requires purchase sizes. The €100 monthly cap requires at least €10 000 / €5 000 of eligible spending, with enough purchases to avoid losses to per-transaction caps.

Paid only in BTC. Kolo fixes its BTC rate at authorization, not payout. Cashback is paid daily for transactions cleared the previous day; EUR clearing can take up to 14 days, USD usually 1–3 days. A completed transaction in the app does not replace the cashback Paid status.

Individual USD / EUR cards only; business and co-branded cards are excluded. No merchant-category restrictions. Refunds before payout reduce cashback. The app shows the highest rate across cards, while each earns its own rate. The potential ranking uses the introductory maximum of $20; this is neither a permanent 2% rate nor confirmed eligibility for my card’s introductory period.

Useful information about the Kolo crypto card

Can I use Kolo Card as a family crypto card?

Share Card lets me give other family members shared access to the card. Everyone I share it with can approve payments. In my experience, this is a convenient and straightforward way to use one card for shared family purchases.

Is the Kolo USD card available in Ukraine?

Kolo offers a USD card, but issuance is unavailable for Ukraine as of November 9, 2026. I therefore use the EUR card in this research and in the Ukrainian scoring scenario. The existence of a USD program does not mean I can use it in Ukraine.

What cashback does Kolo Card offer: 1% or 2%, and what are the caps?

For the EUR card, the introductory 2% lasts only for the first 30 days; the regular rate is then 1%. Cashback is in BTC, with regular-rate caps of €1 per purchase and €100 per month. Large purchases reduce the effective percentage, and received BTC is not a spendable USD balance.

Kolo USDC withdrawals: which networks, fee and minimum apply?

In my recorded terms, the fee is 1 USDC and the transfer minimum depends on the network: 5 USDC on Ethereum, or 1 USDC on BNB Smart Chain, Arbitrum and Base. The fee is added to the transfer amount. Polygon withdrawals are absent from the list I checked.
