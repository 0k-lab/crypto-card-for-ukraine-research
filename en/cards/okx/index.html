Useful information about the OKX crypto card

What exchange rate does OKX Card give for UAH payments in Ukraine?

When the exchange rate for a UAH purchase is the main priority. In the identical 158 UAH test, OKX had the lowest debit before cashback. This is the result of one controlled payment, rather than a guarantee of the best rate for every amount or date.

Does OKX Card USDG cashback work in Ukraine?

USDG is unavailable in my Ukrainian scenario, so its linked cashback and balance yield do not work for me. I do not apply other regions’ terms to my card. In my comparison, the main OKX advantage is therefore the measured cost of the UAH payment.

Does an OKX Card online payment work through RozetkaPay?

RozetkaPay declined OKX Card in my test. I keep this as a practical limitation, but it does not establish rejection by every merchant or permanent incompatibility with the service. For an important purchase, I would keep a backup payment method.

Funding and withdrawing USDT in OKX Pay: is there an X Layer fee?

In my X Layer test, the full 10 USDT was credited and returned to the external wallet without a USDT deduction. I account for the initial sender gas separately. This is the tested X Layer route; Ethereum showed separate swap and transfer costs.
