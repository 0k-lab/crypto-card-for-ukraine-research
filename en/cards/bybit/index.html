Useful information about the Bybit crypto card

Bybit Card in Ukraine: how is the Funding balance used for payments?

In my scenario, the card uses the shared Funding balance, so I do not need a separate manual card-account top-up before paying. I found deposits and withdrawals convenient. The card is also part of the exchange account, which matters when choosing the service.

What is Bybit Card cashback: Base and Beta caps and point redemption?

Bybit cashback has monthly caps and is awarded in points. In the recorded general schedule, Base is capped at 5 USDT, while Beta has separate requirements. The AU program differs, so the active terms must match the specific card; I also distinguish credited points from their redemption.

Does Bybit Card work with Apple Pay and Google Pay?

Yes, Bybit Card supports adding the card and paying through Apple Pay and Google Pay. The inconvenience I encountered concerns a different process: viewing my full card details requires 2FA each time. This adds a step when entering details manually online, rather than requiring 2FA for every phone payment.

Does RozetkaPay accept Bybit Card payments?

RozetkaPay declined Bybit Card in my test. I therefore do not equate a convenient exchange balance and cashback with guaranteed acceptance by every merchant. This is an observed case from my use, rather than a test of every online service.
