How to choose and use a crypto card
Which crypto card offers the best value for payments in Ukraine?

Solid is my current choice for regular use in Ukraine. I would also choose Kolo, especially for family: everyone with shared card access can approve payments. Both have cashback. I would choose OKX for the lowest UAH debit in the controlled test; cashback is unavailable in my Ukrainian scenario. Personal recommendations are separate from the 10-criterion ranking.

Does the displayed purchase cost include deposits and withdrawals?

No. The ranking compares the actual purchase debit, which already includes conversion and fees for that payment. The cashback mode subtracts the included amount. Funding and withdrawals, including conversion during those operations, are assessed separately; network costs depend on the chosen network. Total usage cost depends on the route and transfer amount.

Is the included cashback already available to spend?

Not always. Some cashback is displayed after the purchase, but its payout is still unconfirmed. I value credited Bybit points at the documented redemption ratio without a verified redemption, and BTC at a saved fixed valuation. Each card calculation states the basis for its amount.

How can I get a crypto card in Ukraine?

Choose a card and check availability for your country of residence and the service requirements. Visit the official website, register and complete the required identity verification. Check issuance and ongoing fees before applying. Each card has a non-referral button, and invitation links are labelled separately.

How do crypto payments work in shops and online?

To the merchant, paying with crypto through a crypto card is an ordinary card payment in fiat currency. Fund the required balance using a supported asset and network, then transfer from the wallet to the card if needed. Pay as with a regular payment card: the service uses the crypto balance or previously exchanged funds. Each card’s full cycle and details show the specific route and debits.

Which crypto cards support Apple Pay and Google Pay?

All nine cards in this research support adding the card and paying with Apple Pay and Google Pay. These are Bybit, COCA, KeyOne, Kolo, OKX, Simple, Solid, Trustee Plus and WhiteBIT. Add the card from the service app or through Wallet, enter the card details if needed and complete issuer verification.

Can I spend USDT or USDC with a crypto card?

Yes, the research includes purchases funded with USDT and USDC. The card settlement currency can still be USD or EUR. Supporting a token for deposits does not guarantee that the same token is debited directly for purchases. Exchange rates and fees affect the final amount, so the tests compare the asset actually spent.

How do I fund a crypto card and withdraw the remaining balance?

Open the money-flow diagram on the chosen card page. It separates funding, the payment balance and withdrawals. Before sending funds, match the asset, network, minimum amount and fee against the current in-app terms. Some cards require an internal wallet or exchange transfer. Route costs are shown separately from purchase costs.

About the controlled purchase

9 October 2026 · 9 identical purchases of UAH 158 at one store within a few minutes.

This is one small purchase per card. Differences below one cent may reflect rounding; other amounts and merchants may give different results.

Calculation details

For comparison, I assume 1 USDT ≈ 1 USDC ≈ $1. Card currency, spending asset and internal settlement currency may differ.

  • Debit: before balance minus after balance. Exceptions are USDT spent on the completed OKX screen, Bybit’s USDT debit in asset history, Trustee’s exact receipt and Solid’s Available to spend. Initial balances are kept separate from completed payments.
  • The NBU rate on the test date was 44.8526 ₴/$, giving a $3.522649746… purchase equivalent. Deviation includes visible costs, not only FX spread. I do not add included fees twice.
  • I deduct displayed Solid $0.11 and COCA $0.03 before credit, Bybit’s 36 credited points at 0.002 USDT/point = 0.072 USDT under redemption terms, and Kolo / WhiteBIT BTC at a fixed valuation. Bybit point redemption into USDT is unverified. Trustee Points contribute $0 to the current cash calculation.
  • I value displayed Kolo BTC and credited WhiteBIT BTC at a fixed $82976.035/BTC quote. This is neither spendable USD nor a measured sale rate; each card’s details contain the valuation and source.
