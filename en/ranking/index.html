How scoring works

The best distinct value receives 10 points, the next receives 9. Equal values receive equal scores. The total is the sum of ten scores divided by 10.

Scores express order within this sample. Small and large gaps between adjacent results can produce the same one-point difference. I built this research model for my Ukrainian use case.

Income with no established rate receives the next score below the lowest known result, currently 5. No declared minimum receives 10. Monetary income values remain unknown where no rate is established.

Routes use the lowest confirmed cost with the required starting asset. Gas paid by the external sender is accounted for separately. KeyOne and Trustee funding scores use relative losses from historical tests. Their order for $100 is an assumption.

Clarification on 9 Nov 2026: Simple withdrawal remains provisional (10*): zero USDC deduction was verified in the 11 Oct test, while the current new-wallet cost is unknown. Gasless does not mean free transfers. WhiteBIT payment scoring uses the measured 9 Oct purchase; current fees are not added on top of the measured debit. Positions and numerical totals remain unchanged.

Solid uses the displayed 8% APY with unknown inclusion of the 10% performance fee. COCA uses 5% APY and assumes all $1,000 is eligible because the cap wording conflicts. Simple uses 5% APR. Rates are variable and these are modeled accruals.

Simple includes 8 embedded-wallet networks. The Solid USDC withdrawal screen confirms Arbitrum, Base, BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum and Fuse. Polygon is marked coming soon and is excluded from the network count.

Kolo pays BTC and WhiteBIT rewards selected categories. Values use nominal valuation before any later sale. Bybit remains on Base without a mid-month Beta upgrade. Withdrawal from Solid Savings may carry a separate 0.01% fee.

Income

Income is modeled for $1,000 spent evenly to zero over 30 days, giving an average balance of $500. Rates remain constant in the model. APR and APY use separate calculations.

Bybit USDT pays 7.34% APR on the first 100 and 2.34% above that. Average amounts in those portions are 95 and 405. Income = (95 × 0.0734 + 405 × 0.0234) × 30 / 365, approximately $1.35.

Cashback

Cashback assumes 20 eligible $50 purchases at the base tier, after any required waiting period. WhiteBIT uses the agreed 1% in a selected category. Welcome and referral rewards are considered separately.
